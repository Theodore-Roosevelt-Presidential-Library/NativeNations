# Wording questions for nations (drafts)

Each is short, asks one or two specific things, and offers removal. Send to the nation's tourism or cultural office if one is listed; otherwise its general contact.

## Flandreau Santee Sioux Tribe (fsst-nsn.gov)

**Subject:** One wording question about the Flandreau Santee Sioux Tribe's history

Hello,

The Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library is drafting a directory page for the Flandreau Santee Sioux Tribe that links visitors to the Tribe's own sites: https://nativenations.labs.trlibrary.com/nations/flandreau-santee-sioux-tribe-of-south-dakota/

Its short land history describes what happened to Dakota people after the 1862 war. The Tribe's own website uses the term "concentration camps"; the draft says "imprisoned." Which wording would the Tribe like used? And is there anything else on the page the Tribe would like changed or removed? The Tribe's own account governs.

## Fort Belknap Indian Community (ftbelknap.org)

**Subject:** A date question for the Fort Belknap page

The draft land history for Fort Belknap mentions the cession of land in the Little Rocky Mountains. The community's website dates it 1885; the federal statute and Montana's Office of Public Instruction date the agreement 1895–96. Which date does the Fort Belknap Indian Community use? Draft page: https://nativenations.labs.trlibrary.com/nations/fort-belknap-indian-community-of-the-fort-belknap-reservation-of/

## Crow Tribe (crow-nsn.gov)

**Subject:** How should the Crow Tribe's land history describe its claims?

The draft land history describes the Tribe's long effort to be fairly paid for land taken in the nineteenth century, using wording from Montana's Office of Public Instruction. The Library would rather use the Tribe's own words. Is there a statement or page the Tribe would like cited, or wording it prefers? Draft page: https://nativenations.labs.trlibrary.com/nations/crow-tribe-of-montana/

## Blackfeet Nation (blackfeetnation.com)

**Subject:** Two questions for the Blackfeet Nation page

1. The draft land history describes the 1895–96 agreement that took the mountains along the reservation's western edge, using wording from Montana's Office of Public Instruction. Is there a Nation statement the Library should cite instead?
2. The draft lists Badger-Two Medicine as a place the Nation names sacred, based on the Nation's own news posts. It is hidden until the Nation confirms. Would the Nation like it shown, and with what wording?

Draft page: https://nativenations.labs.trlibrary.com/nations/blackfeet-tribe-of-the-blackfeet-indian-reservation-of-montana/
